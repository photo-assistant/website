Built for your home

A photo system, not just a gallery.

One library, many sources

Your NAS, your phone, that old laptop and your external drives, all in one timeline — without moving or duplicating a single file.

Private by design

Free and open source. No tracking, no accounts with us, no cloud required. Your memories never leave your home unless you say so.

AI that runs on your hardware

Smart search, face grouping and text recognition happen on your own server. Even a small mini PC can do it.

Every screen is a frame

Turn your TV into a living photo wall, send an album to the kitchen tablet, or keep a digital frame always fresh.

Made for Home Assistant

Use your photos in automations and dashboards: show the kids' photos when they get home, or this day's memories every morning.

Clean up the clutter

Find duplicates, screenshots and memes from chat apps automatically, so the moments that matter rise to the top.
